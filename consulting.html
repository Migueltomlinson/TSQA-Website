What We Offer

Consulting Services

From a single site inspection to a full safety management system build — TSQA covers every stage of your HSEQ journey.

01 Risk & Safety Management

Risk Assessments

Comprehensive hazard identification, risk evaluation, and control development — from simple workplace assessments to complex multi-site environments.

Workplace Safety Inspections

Thorough on-site inspection by experienced HSEQ professionals — identifying gaps before they become incidents or enforcement actions.

Site Safety Plans & Risk Assessments

Site-specific safety plans and documented risk assessments that meet principal contractor, client, and regulatory requirements.

Safety Management Systems Development

Design and implementation of structured SMS frameworks — policies, procedures, registers, and systems that actually work in your organisation.

Emergency Preparedness & Response Plans

Practical, site-relevant emergency plans developed with your team — covering scenarios, roles, communications, and evacuation procedures.

Emergency Drills

Facilitated emergency drill programmes that test your plans, build staff confidence, and meet legislative and insurance requirements.

02 Incident & Culture Improvement

Incident & Accident Investigation

Independent, thorough investigation using structured methodologies — identifying root causes, contributing factors, and effective corrective actions.

Safety Culture Assessment

Structured assessment of your organisation's safety culture — measuring maturity, identifying barriers, and benchmarking against industry expectations.

Safety Culture Improvement

Targeted interventions, leadership coaching, and programme design that shifts culture from reactive to genuinely proactive — measurably and sustainably.

03 Contractor & Pre-Qualification Support

Contractor & Supplier Safety Management

End-to-end contractor safety frameworks — from prequalification criteria and onboarding through to ongoing performance monitoring and review.

Pre-Qualification Services

Expert support to achieve and maintain pre-qualification with IMPAC, SiteWise, SHE, and Tōtika — the systems required by New Zealand's major principals.

04 Auditing & Compliance

Internal Audits

Structured internal audits against your own management system, legislative requirements, or recognised standards — with clear findings and prioritised actions.

Supplier / External Audits

Second-party audit services to evaluate supplier and subcontractor performance — giving you confidence in your supply chain's safety and quality standards.

Hazardous Substances Registers & SDS Management

Compliant hazardous substance registers and safety data sheet management systems — meeting HSNO and WorkSafe New Zealand requirements.

05 Quality Assurance & Quality Control

ISO 9001:2015 QMS Implementation

Full design and implementation of an ISO 9001-compliant quality management system — from gap analysis through to certification readiness.

Inspection & Test Plan (ITP) Development

Tailored ITPs that define inspection points, test methods, acceptance criteria, and responsible parties — ensuring quality is built in, not inspected in.

Manufacturing Data Report (MDR) Compilation

Structured compilation of MDRs for engineered products — consolidating test records, certificates, and inspection data into compliant documentation packages.

Visual Weld Inspection

Qualified visual weld inspection services for structural, pressure, and pipeline applications — ensuring welds meet specification before they leave the shop floor.

WPS Verification & Welder Qualifications

Welding Procedure Specification review and welder qualification assessment — confirming that procedures and personnel meet applicable standards.

Material Traceability & ITR Management

End-to-end material traceability systems and Inspection and Test Record management — providing the audit trail your projects and principals require.
